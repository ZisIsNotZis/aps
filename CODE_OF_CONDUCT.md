# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want APS to be a welcoming place for experimentation and technical
disagreement. Treat people with respect, assume good faith, explain your
reasoning, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than individuals.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and
deliberate disruption are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact
mechanism on GitHub. Maintainers may clarify, remove content, or restrict
participation when needed to keep the project constructive.
